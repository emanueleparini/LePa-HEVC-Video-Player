# Privacy Policy — LePa HEVC Player

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

LePa HEVC Player is published by LePa s.r.l. (Cesenatico, Italy). This policy covers the Windows app
distributed through GitHub and the Microsoft Store.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting
service. It does not send any data to LePa s.r.l. or to third parties.

## Data stored on your device

The app keeps a few files on your own PC, in `%AppData%\LePa HEVC Player\`:

- `settings.json`: volume and the list of recently opened files;
- `error.log`: details of unexpected errors, written only if one occurs.

Screenshots you take with the app are saved to `Pictures\LePa HEVC Player\`.

These files never leave your device. You can delete them at any time; uninstalling the app does not
remove them automatically.

## Network access

The app connects to the internet only when you explicitly open a network stream (File > Open URL / stream…).
In that case it connects directly to the address you entered, and that server's own privacy policy applies.
